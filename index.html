Doctor Stock

Inventory assistant for a medical practice.

Doctor Stock is a private Telegram bot that helps the staff of a medical practice keep track of their supplies. Authorized users send text messages or photos (purchase receipts, products, printed stock counts), and the bot records purchases, transfers, usage and stock counts.

Inventory data is stored in a Google Sheet, and receipt photos in a Google Drive folder. Both belong to the practice's own Google account, and the app only accesses files it created itself.

The bot is not public: it only answers users the practice has authorized.
