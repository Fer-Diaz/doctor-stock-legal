Privacy Policy

Doctor Stock · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Doctor Stock is a private Telegram bot that manages the supply inventory of a medical practice. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and who it is shared with.

Who can use the app

Only users the practice has authorized can use the bot. Messages from any other Telegram account are not processed: the bot only replies with that account's Telegram ID so an administrator can authorize it.

Data the app handles

  • Telegram identity: each authorized user's Telegram ID and name.
  • Messages and photos: the text messages and photos users send to the bot (purchase receipts, products and printed stock counts).
  • Inventory records: products, quantities, expiration dates, suppliers, prices, receipt numbers and the log of every stock movement, including which user made it.

The app does not handle patient data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to operate the inventory: understanding what a user asked for, recording the change once the user confirms it, answering stock questions and sending daily stock alerts to users who opted in.

Where the data is stored

  • Google Sheets and Google Drive: inventory records are stored in a Google Sheet, and receipt photos in a Google Drive folder. Both belong to the practice's own Google account. Product photos and stock-count photos are processed and then discarded, not stored.
  • Microsoft Azure: the app runs on Microsoft Azure. Azure stores the app's credentials, the user's pending confirmation, and technical logs used to detect errors.

Use of Google user data

The app requests a single Google permission, drive.file. With it, the app can only see and change files that it created itself (the inventory Sheet and the receipt photos folder). It cannot see any other file in the Google account.

Doctor Stock's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Third-party services

  • Telegram: delivers messages between users and the bot.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): interprets messages and reads photos. Anthropic processes this content to return a response and does not use it to train its models.
  • Google: stores the inventory Sheet and receipt photos.
  • Microsoft Azure: hosts the app.

Data is not shared with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Inventory records and receipt photos are kept for as long as the practice uses the app, because the movement log is the inventory's history. The practice can delete them at any time from its Google account. Technical logs are deleted automatically after a limited period.

To remove a user's access or ask for their data to be deleted, contact the address below.
